## 📌 1. Introduction & Objectives

Pollution hamare environment ke liye sabse bada khatra hai. Ye plants, animals, aur humans sabko equally affect karti hai. Is unit mein hum 3 main types ki pollution padhenge: **Air, Water, aur Soil**.

**PDF ke Important Points:**
*   **Air Pollution:** Harmful gases aur particles ka air mein milna. WHO ne 4 main categories banayi hain: **Particulate Matter, Ozone, Nitrogen Dioxide, Sulphur Dioxide**.
*   **Primary vs Secondary Pollutants:** 
    *   *Primary:* Direct source se (e.g., CO from vehicles).
    *   *Secondary:* Chemical reaction se bante hain (e.g., Ozone, Smog).
*   **Water Pollution:** Water essential hai, par freshwater limited hai. Population, industries, agriculture se water depletion ho rahi hai.
*   **Study Note (PDF):** Duniya mein sabse zyada log **road accidents, wars, etc. se nahi, balki contaminated water peene se** marte hain.
*   **Soil Pollution:** Autotrophs (plants) chemicals aur sewage se affect hote hain, jo phir herbivores aur humans tak pahunchta hai.

**Learning Objectives:**
*   Air pollution ke sources aur effects samajhna.
*   Water pollution ke sources (Point & Non-Point) aur health effects samajhna.
*   Soil pollution ke sources, effects, aur control measures samajhna.

## 🌫️ 2. Air Pollution (Complete)

**Definition:** Jab air mein harmful gases aur particles (solid/liquid) mil jate hain, toh use Air Pollution kehte hain. Ye human health aur environment dono ko nuksan pahunchata hai.

### 2.1 Types of Air Pollutants
1.  **Particulate Matter (PM):** Solid aur liquid particles ka mixture (sulphate, nitrates, carbon, dust, soot, smoke). Size ke hisaab se alag hote hain.
2.  **Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2):** Vehicles, heating plants, aur power stations se nikalta hai. Diesel vehicles main source hain.
3.  **Sulphur Dioxide (SO2):** Colourless gas with suffocating smell. Coal aur oil burn hone se nikalta hai. Mainly industries se.

> [!NOTE]
> **PDF Study Note:** Chemical reactions, agricultural activities, aur transportation air contamination ke primary sources hain. Toxins ka clustering sirf dispersion ya breakdown se kam hota hai jab wo environment mein release hote hain.

### 2.2 Negative Impact of Air Pollution (PDF ke according)
*   **Health Issues:** Respiratory diseases, coronary illness, **COPD (Chronic Pulmonary Obstructive Disorder)**, stroke, lung cancer.
*   **WHO Stats (2016):** Ambient (outdoor) air pollution se **4.2 million premature deaths** worldwide.
*   **WHO Total:** Approx **7 million people** lose their lives due to air pollution.
*   **Economic Cost:** Institute of Health Metrics and Evaluation aur World Bank ne estimate kiya ki **US$5 trillion** welfare losses hui hain.
*   **Children:** Contaminated zones ke paas rehne wale bachche **pneumonia aur asthma** ke zyada shikar hote hain.

### 2.3 Sources of Air Pollution (4 Types)

| Source Type | Description | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Mobile Sources** | Jo ek jagah se dusri jagah move karte hain. | Cars, buses, trucks, airplanes. (CO release karte hain) |
| **2. Stationary Sources** | Jo ek hi jagah fix rehte hain. | Power plants, petroleum refineries, industrial facilities. |
| **3. Area Sources** | Jo specific region mein hote hain. | Farming areas, urban areas, wood-burning chimneys. |
| **4. Natural Sources** | Jo nature se hote hain, inko control nahi kar sakte. | Volcanic eruptions, wind-blown dust, forest fires, ocean salt spray. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **PDF Fact:** 2016 WHO statistics ke according, **India top 5 polluted countries** mein hai jahan **10+ cities** mein highest pollution hai.

**Toxic Emissions ke Causes (PDF):**
*   Chemical variations
*   Unusual changes in environment
*   Exploitation of resources
*   Global obligations
*   Climate aur geography ka effect

### 2.4 Effects of Air Pollution

#### A. On Environment
*   **Global Warming:** CO2 aur Methane ki high emission se earth ka temperature badh raha hai. Manufacturing industry main source hai.
*   **Climate Change:** Temperature rise se climatic cycles disturb ho rahe hain.
*   **Acid Rain:** SO2 aur NOx gases paani ke saath mil kar acid rain banati hain. Ye soil, water bodies, aur buildings ko nuksan pahunchati hai.
*   **Smog Effect:** Urban areas mein toxic haze. 2 types:
    *   *Sulphurous Smog:* Coal burning se (London type).
    *   *Photochemical Smog:* Vehicles aur sunlight se (Los Angeles type).
*   **Deterioration of Fields:** Acid rain se soil ki composition badal jati hai, jo agriculture aur food chain ko affect karta hai.

#### B. On Human Health
*   **Short-term:** Respiratory discomfort, cardiovascular issues, asthma attacks, heart aur lungs par pressure.
*   **Long-term:** Lung capacity kam hona, early aging of lungs, bronchitis, emphysema, lung cancer, life expectancy kam hona.

### 2.5 Control Measures for Air Pollution (5 Steps - PDF)
1.  **Public Transport:** Vehicle emissions kam karne ke liye.
2.  **Alternative Fuels:** Electric aur solar vehicles use karna.
3.  **Eco-friendly Packaging:** Plastic burn karne se toxins nikalte hain, isliye biodegradable use karein.
4.  **Avoid Fireworks:** Ye CO aur toxins release karte hain.
5.  **Afforestation:** Zyada ped lagana (oxygen badhta hai, AQI improve hota hai).

## 💧 3. Water Pollution (Complete)

**Definition:** Jab harmful chemicals, biological components, ya hot water (thermal pollution) freshwater bodies mein mil jate hain, toh use Water Pollution kehte hain.

*   **Temperature Effect:** Hot water discharge se aquatic life ko nuksan hota hai, dissolved oxygen kam hota hai. Eggs aur larvae temperature changes ke liye susceptible hote hain.

### 3.1 Sources of Water Pollution

#### A. Point Sources (Single Source)
Pollution ek specific jagah se aati hai (drainpipe, factory, sewage plant).
*   **Groundwater Pollutants:** Organic compounds (oils, paints, solvents, fuels). Ek baar groundwater pollute hua toh clean karna mushkil hai.
*   **Surface Water Pollutants:** High-temperature discharges, Nutrients (Phosphorus, Nitrogen), Microorganisms (bacteria, viruses, Giardia).

#### B. Non-Point Sources (Diffuse Sources)
Pollution ka koi single source nahi hota. Ye water ke flow ke saath land se aati hai (runoff).
*   **Sediment:** Soil erosion se. Water ki turbidity badhati hai, light kam hone se aquatic plants affect hote hain.
*   **Nutrients:** Fertilizers se (Nitrogen & Phosphorus) → **Eutrophication** (algae bloom, oxygen kam hona).
*   **Pathogens:** Bacteria/Viruses (Salmonella, Giardia, Cryptosporidium) from animal waste.
*   **Toxic Contaminants:** Heavy metals (Lead, Mercury, Zinc, Cadmium), Pesticides, PAHs, PCBs.

**Non-Point Sources by Land Use:**
*   **Urban:** Storm water (oil, dust, pet waste).
*   **Agricultural:** Fertilizers, pesticides, animal dung runoff.
*   **Forestry:** Soil erosion due to logging.
*   **Marinas/Boating:** Fuel spills, paint chemicals.

> [!NOTE]
> **PDF Study Note:** India mein water pollution ka sabse bada source **"Untreated Sewage"** hai. Other sources: unregulated small-scale industry waste, agricultural runoff.

### 3.2 Effects of Water Pollution

#### On Environment
*   **Eutrophication (Dead Zones):** Excess nutrients se algae badhti hai, oxygen khatam hota hai, marine life mar jati hai.
*   **Marine Ecosystem Damage:** Plastic waste, fishing nets se animals choke hote hain.
*   **Ocean Acidification:** CO2 absorb hone se ocean ka pH kam hota hai, jo corals aur shellfish ke liye khatarnak hai.

#### On Human Health (PDF heading hai par content cut hai, standard exam points)
*   **Waterborne Diseases:** Cholera, Typhoid, Dysentery, Hepatitis A.
*   **Skin Infections:** Contact with polluted water.
*   **Heavy Metal Poisoning:** Mercury, Lead, Arsenic se organ damage.
*   **Study Note (PDF):** Duniya mein sabse zyada log contaminated water peene se marte hain.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Extra Exam Topic:** **BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand):** Kitni oxygen bacteria ko organic matter decompose karne mein lagti hai. High BOD = High Pollution.
> **COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand):** Chemicals ko oxidize karne ke liye kitni oxygen lagti hai.

### 3.3 Control Measures for Water Pollution
1.  **Sewage Treatment:** Untreated sewage ko water bodies mein mat chhodo.
2.  **Industrial Effluent Treatment:** Factories ko treatment plant lagana chahiye.
3.  **Reduce, Reuse, Recycle:** Water ko waste mat karo.
4.  **Organic Farming:** Fertilizers aur pesticides ka use kam karo.
5.  **Afforestation:** Trees water cycle maintain karte hain aur soil erosion rokate hain.

## 🪨 4. Soil Pollution (Complete)

> [!WARNING]
> PDF mein Soil Pollution section cut ho gaya tha (page 24 ke baad missing hai), isliye maine standard exam concepts se ise complete kiya hai.

**Definition:** Soil mein harmful chemicals ya substances ka milna jo uski fertility aur ecosystem ko nuksan pahunchaye.

### 4.1 Sources of Soil Pollution
1.  **Industrial Waste:** Factories se nikalne wala toxic waste (heavy metals, chemicals) soil mein milta hai.
2.  **Agricultural Practices:** Chemical fertilizers, pesticides, aur insecticides ka overuse.
3.  **Urbanization:** Construction waste, plastic, aur garbage.
4.  **Mining:** Mining se heavy metals aur chemicals soil mein aate hain.
5.  **Acid Rain:** SO2 aur NOx gases se acid rain hoti hai jo soil ko acidic banati hai.

### 4.2 Effects of Soil Pollution
1.  **Loss of Fertility:** Soil ki quality kharab hoti hai, crops nahi ugte.
2.  **Bioaccumulation/Biomagnification:** Chemicals plants mein jate hain, phir animals aur humans mein (food chain ke through). Ye cancer aur organ damage kar sakta hai.
3.  **Water Pollution:** Soil ke pollutants groundwater mein mil jate hain.
4.  **Air Pollution:** Dust aur volatile chemicals hawa mein mil jate hain.

### 4.3 Control Measures
1.  **Crop Rotation:** Alag-alag crops ugana (soil nutrients maintain rehte hain).
2.  **Organic Fertilizers:** Chemical fertilizers ki jagah compost aur manure use karna.
3.  **Bioremediation:** Bacteria/fungi se soil ke pollutants ko clean karna.
4.  **Proper Waste Disposal:** Industries ko treatment plant lagana chahiye.
5.  **Afforestation:** Trees soil erosion rokate hain aur fertility maintain karte hain.

## 📝 5. Summary, Cheat Sheet & Q&A

### 5.1 Summary (PDF ke according)
*   **Air Pollution:** Gases (SO2, NO2, CO, Ozone) aur particles (PM) se hota hai. **WHO** ne 4 categories banayi hain. Sources: Mobile, Stationary, Area, Natural. Effects: Global warming, acid rain, smog, health issues (asthma, lung cancer). Control: Public transport, afforestation, eco-friendly products.
*   **Water Pollution:** Point (single source) aur Non-Point (diffuse) sources hote hain. **Eutrophication**, ocean acidification, marine life damage karta hai. India mein main source **Untreated Sewage** hai. Control: Sewage treatment, industrial effluent treatment.
*   **Soil Pollution:** Industrial waste, fertilizers, acid rain se hota hai. Fertility kam karta hai, **bioaccumulation** karta hai. Control: Crop rotation, bioremediation, organic farming.
